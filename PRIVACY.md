# Privacy Policy · 隐私政策

**Floating Island Bookmarks (浮岛书签 Visual Bookmark)**
Last updated: October 17, 2026

[English](#english) · [中文](#中文)

## English

Floating Island Bookmarks is a Chrome extension for organizing your bookmarks. It runs entirely on your computer.

### What data the extension handles

- **Bookmarks.** The extension reads your bookmarks through Chrome's `bookmarks` API to display and organize them. It changes a bookmark only when you drag, edit, delete, or create one yourself.
- **Site icons.** Icons are read from Chrome's built-in local `favicon` service. No request is made to any third-party icon service.
- **Interface preferences.** Settings such as background, transparency, and open panels are stored in your browser's local storage. A custom background image you choose is stored in your browser's local IndexedDB.

### What the extension does not do

- It does not collect, upload, sell, or share any data with anyone.
- It has no server, no account, and no analytics or advertising code.
- It does not load remote code. All code ships inside the extension package.

### Removing data

Uninstalling the extension removes all of its settings and any background image you added. Your bookmarks belong to Chrome and are not affected by uninstalling.

### Contact

Questions can be filed as an issue on GitHub: https://github.com/Freedomshine/chromevisualbookmark/issues

## 中文

浮岛书签（Visual Bookmark）是一个在本机运行的 Chrome 书签整理扩展。

### 处理哪些数据

- **书签**：扩展通过 Chrome 的 `bookmarks` 接口读取你的书签，用来显示和整理。只有在你主动拖拽、编辑、删除或新建时，才会修改对应的书签。
- **网站图标**：通过 Chrome 内置的 `favicon` 服务在本地读取，不向任何第三方图标服务发请求。
- **界面设置**：背景、透明度、打开的面板等偏好保存在本机浏览器的本地存储里；你选择的自定义背景图片保存在本机的 IndexedDB 中。

### 不做的事

- 不收集、不上传、不出售任何数据，也不与任何第三方共享。
- 没有服务器，没有账号，没有统计或广告代码。
- 不加载任何远程代码，所有代码都随扩展一起打包。

### 删除数据

卸载扩展即可清除它保存的全部设置和背景图片。你的书签属于 Chrome，本身不受卸载影响。

### 联系

有问题可以在 GitHub 提 issue：https://github.com/Freedomshine/chromevisualbookmark/issues
